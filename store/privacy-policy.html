BCS Study Plan: Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · App ID com.iamnazmul.bcs_study_plan

BCS Study Plan is a 120-day study planner for the BCS Foreign Affairs cadre exam. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the developer collects nothing, and your data stays on your phone.

Data stored on your device

Your study plan, sessions, progress and settings are saved in a local database on your phone. They are never sent to the developer or to any server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Calendar access (optional)

If you choose Connect calendar, the app asks for permission to read and write your device calendars. It uses this permission only to:

  • list the calendars on your phone so that you can pick one;
  • add, update and remove events for your study sessions in the calendar you picked;
  • check whether events it created earlier still exist.

The app does not read, copy or upload your other calendar events. If the calendar you pick belongs to a Google account, Android syncs those study events to Google Calendar under Google's own privacy policy, as it does for any calendar event. You can disconnect at any time from the app's Google Calendar screen, which removes the study events. You can also revoke calendar permission in Android Settings.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.

Children

The app is meant for adult exam candidates and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
